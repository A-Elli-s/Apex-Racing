Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

Apex Racing World is an independent motorsport information website. This policy explains information that may be processed when you use the site and how to contact us about privacy.

Information we collect

You can browse without creating an account. The website does not currently provide account registration. When you contact us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, name, message and any links you share. Please do not send passwords, payment card numbers, sensitive personal information or confidential materials.

Our hosting and infrastructure providers may automatically process technical information, such as IP address, device/browser information, requested pages, timestamps and security logs, to deliver and protect the site. We do not claim that such logs are never retained.

How information is used

We use contact information to respond to inquiries, review suggestions, correct errors, investigate abuse and improve the website. Technical data may be used for performance, reliability and security. We do not sell contact submissions or use them to send marketing messages without an appropriate basis.

Third-party services and links

Cloudflare may process technical data to host and secure the website. Racing schedules, standings, images or other content may be requested from third-party services, including public motorsport data providers, image hosts and Wikimedia Commons; those providers may receive technical request information. Links to organizers, streaming services, ticket providers and stores take you to websites governed by their own privacy notices.

Affiliate links

Some shopping links contain affiliate tracking identifiers, including Amazon Associates links. A retailer may use cookies or similar technologies to attribute purchases after you follow a link. We do not receive your full payment details from the retailer. Affiliate commissions do not increase the price you pay through our site.

Cookies and analytics

We do not currently describe or operate an account-based tracking program on this site. Essential infrastructure or third-party embedded resources may nevertheless use cookies or similar technologies. If we add dedicated analytics, advertising pixels, a hosted contact form or marketing tools, this notice will be updated and consent mechanisms added where required.

Sharing and retention

Information may be processed by hosting, email and security providers as needed to operate the site. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect the service. Contact messages are kept only as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain records, address disputes and comply with legal obligations; specific retention periods may vary with the inquiry and provider.

Your choices and rights

You may choose not to provide contact information and may avoid following external links. Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or other restrictions on personal information. Contact us using the contact page to make a request; we may need to verify your identity before acting.

Children

This site is intended for a general motorsport audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child submitted personal information, please contact us.

Security and changes

We take reasonable steps to protect the site, but no internet transmission or storage method is completely secure. We may revise this policy as our features or providers change. The effective date above indicates the latest revision.

For questions about this page, visit our contact page.
